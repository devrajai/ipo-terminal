IPO Tips
  • Read the DRHP/RHP — business overview, risk factors, financials
  • Check revenue growth and profitability
  • Compare valuation with listed peers
  • Check ROE, ROCE, debt and cash flow
  • Verify promoter holding and IPO structure
  • Use cut-off price where appropriate
  • Approve UPI mandate promptly
  • Apply before the closing rush
  • Don't rely only on GMP — it is unofficial
  • Don't apply blindly to every IPO
  • Don't bid below cut-off when using a cut-off bid
  • Don't use a third-party UPI ID
  • Don't submit multiple applications with the same PAN
  • Don't ignore valuation or risk factors
  • Don't confuse hype with fundamentals
  • Don't invest money needed immediately
  • Track GMP trend rather than a single number
  • Check anchor investor quality
  • Review HNI and retail subscription data
  • Check market sentiment alongside IPO fundamentals
  • Use ASBA for large bids where suitable
  • Watch promoter selling and OFS details
  • Declining revenue or weak operating performance
  • Persistent negative operating cash flow
  • Large valuation premium without clear justification
  • Low post-issue promoter holding
  • Very large OFS component
  • High debt or significant related-party transactions
  • Rapidly falling GMP during subscription

IPO Glossary
IPO
Initial Public Offering
The first public issue of shares by a company.
DRHP
Draft Red Herring Prospectus
Draft offer document filed before the IPO process.
RHP
Red Herring Prospectus
Offer document containing key IPO information and risk disclosures.
GMP
Grey Market Premium
Unofficial market indication that is not regulated by SEBI or the exchanges.
ASBA
Application Supported by Blocked Amount
IPO application method where funds remain blocked in the bank account until allocation.
Cut-off
Highest eligible bid price
Retail investors can choose the cut-off option in book-built issues where permitted.
QIB
Qualified Institutional Buyer
Institutional investor category defined under applicable securities rules.
HNI / NII
Non-Institutional Investor
Investor category outside retail and QIB categories.
Lot Size
Minimum number of shares
The minimum quantity of shares that can generally be applied for in one retail lot.
OFS
Offer for Sale
Existing shareholders sell shares to the public as part of an issue.
