Tips, Tricks & Pro Strategies
✅ DO's
  • Read the DRHP/RHP - business overview, risk factors, financials
  • Check revenue growth - look for 15-25%+ YoY growth over 3 years
  • Verify profitability - profitable in 2 of last 3 years
  • Compare P/E with listed peers - avoid if 2x peers with no justification
  • Check ROE & ROCE - both above 15% for quality companies
  • Examine debt - D/E below 0.5 is comfortable, above 1 is risky
  • Verify cash flow - operating CF should be close to PAT
  • Check promoter holding - above 50% post-IPO shows skin in game
  • Use cut-off price - maximizes allotment chances
  • Approve UPI mandate immediately - within 30 min
  • Apply 24-48 hrs before close - avoid last-minute congestion
  • Use family accounts - different PANs = more lottery chances
❌ DON'Ts
  • Don't rely only on GMP - unofficial, unregulated, can be wrong
  • Don't apply for every IPO blindly - subscription != value
  • Don't bid below cut-off - reduces allotment probability
  • Don't use third-party UPI ID - PAN must match demat holder
  • Don't apply multiple times with same PAN - all rejected
  • Don't ignore valuation - good company at wrong price = bad investment
  • Don't confuse hype with quality - social media buzz != fundamentals
  • Don't invest money you need in 7-10 days - funds stay blocked
  • Don't skip risk factors - they contain the most important warnings
  • Don't forget 20% STCG tax - selling within 12 months = 20% tax
💡 PRO TIPS
  • Multiple family Demat accounts - multiply allotment chances
  • GMP trend matters more than absolute GMP - rising = strengthening
  • Check anchor investor quality - which MFs/institutions invested
  • Sell on listing day for momentum plays - book profits if GMP high
  • Hold quality IPOs 1+ year for LTCG (12.5% tax, Rs 1.25L exempt)
  • Pre-apply feature - apply before IPO officially opens
  • Check HNI subscription - if >10x, expect strong listing
  • Market sentiment check - bear markets = even good IPOs list at discount
  • Use ASBA for large bids - UPI cap is Rs 5L
  • Watch promoter selling - large OFS = question why they're exiting
⚠ RED FLAGS - AVOID
  • Revenue growth below 5% or declining
  • Profits but negative operating cash flow consistently
  • P/E 2x+ above peers with no justification
  • Promoter holding post-IPO below 30%
  • Large OFS (>80%) - promoters cashing out
  • D/E above 1.0 with IPO proceeds going to debt repayment
  • ROE and ROCE both below 10%
  • Heavy related-party transactions with promoter entities
  • GMP falling rapidly during subscription period

IPO Glossary
GMP
Grey Market Premium - unofficial price at which IPO shares trade before listing. Unregulated, not guaranteed.
P/E Ratio
Price-to-Earnings ratio. Compare with listed peers to judge if IPO is expensive.
ROE
Return on Equity. Net profit / shareholder's equity. Above 15% is good.
ROCE
Return on Capital Employed. Above 15% is good.
EBITDA
Earnings Before Interest, Tax, Depreciation & Amortisation.
D/E Ratio
Debt-to-Equity. Below 0.5 is comfortable, above 1.0 is risky.
Fresh Issue
Company issues NEW shares. Money goes TO the company.
OFS
Offer for Sale. Existing shareholders sell. Company gets NOTHING.
Lot Size
Minimum shares you must apply for.
Cut-off Price
Agree to buy at final price. Always choose for retail.
ASBA
Application Supported by Blocked Amount. Money stays in bank, blocked.
DRHP
Draft Red Herring Prospectus. Preliminary document filed with SEBI.
RHP
Red Herring Prospectus. Final document with price band, dates.
STCG Tax
Selling within 12 months = 20% tax on profit.
LTCG Tax
Holding 12+ months = 12.5% tax (Rs 1.25L exempt).
Anchor Investors
Institutional investors who invest Rs 10+ crore before IPO opens.
