💡 IPO Tips
Do

Read the RHP / DRHP, financials, valuation, risks and use of proceeds.

Don't

Do not treat GMP or social-media claims as guaranteed listing returns.

Red flags

Check debt, cash flow, promoter holding, related parties and valuation.
